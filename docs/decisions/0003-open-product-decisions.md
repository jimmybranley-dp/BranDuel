# ADR 0003: product decisions resolved

Status: Accepted — product owner decisions recorded September 17, 2026

The product owner resolved the previously open questions as follows:

- support up to four concurrent markets;
- remove scheduling from the product, while retaining legacy scheduled snapshots only for safe recovery/void handling;
- require Jimmy to start Game Night while allowing signed-in players to operate normal rounds;
- settle the official win in person rather than having the app declare it;
- use full rating weight for prep mode.

The current contract and implementation evidence are maintained in [PRODUCT.md](../PRODUCT.md#unresolved-product-decisions). The code/tests and this record now treat these as settled product decisions.
